The Programme

Three weeks. One standard at a time.

This is not another overhaul. It is a structured, graduated process built around curiosity, not criticism.

Week One

Notice

You'll experiment with seven daily standards — not to master them, but to observe yourself. Which felt easy? Which created resistance? What stories did your brain tell you? This week is about understanding yourself.

Week Two

Prove

You'll choose the easiest standard from Week One. Just one. Because before we tackle the hardest thing, we need evidence. Evidence that you can keep promises to yourself. This is where self-trust begins.

Week Three

Follow the Resistance

You'll choose the standard that felt hardest. Not to force yourself — because that's where the learning is. The habits aren't the transformation. Understanding why you resist them is.

Questions

Do I have to complete all seven standards every day?

No. In Week One, you'll experiment with one standard each day to discover which feel natural and which create resistance. In Weeks Two and Three, you'll focus on just one standard at a time. Simple, achievable and personalised.

What if I miss a day?

That's not failure — that's data. One of the core principles of The Munday Method is curiosity over criticism. If you miss a day, we'll use it to understand what happened and how you want to respond next time.

How long are the daily videos?

Most are around 5–10 minutes, making them easy to fit into even the busiest schedule.

I'm already pretty healthy. Will I still benefit?

If you've let the basics slip after a busy season, absolutely. This isn't about starting from scratch — it's about getting back to the version of you that feels energised, focused and consistent.

Will I have lifetime access?

Yes. In fact, I hope you come back to this every time life gets busy and you feel yourself drifting from your standards.
